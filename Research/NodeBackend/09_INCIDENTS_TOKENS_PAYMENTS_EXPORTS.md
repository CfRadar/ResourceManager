# Step 09: Incidents, Dispute Ruling, Tokens, Invoicing & Exports

This guide details incident reporting, appeals, the append-only token ledger, simulated invoicing, and report document generation.

---

## 1. Incidents & Disputes (`/incidents`)

Handles equipment damage, safety violations, late return disputes, or scheduling conflicts (Section 4.16).

### 1.1 Incident Flow
```
[REPORTED] ──> [UNDER_REVIEW] <───> [INFO_REQUESTED]
                     │
                     ▼
             [RESOLVED / DISMISSED]
                     │ (appeal filed)
                     ▼
                 [APPEALED] ──> [FINAL_RULING]
```

### 1.2 Core Rules
- **Right of Reply:** Every party can submit notes, counter-evidence, and witness statements.
- **Internal vs Shared Notes:** Notes have `visibility: 'INTERNAL'` (staff only) or `'SHARED'` (visible to user).
- **Impartiality:** The ruling manager cannot be a party to the incident.
- **AI Draft Assistance:** Managers can invoke `POST /incidents/:id/draft-summary` to get an objective factual summary generated by the Python AI service.

---

## 2. Fair-Use Token Ledger (`/tokens`)

An internal credit allocation mechanism preventing lab monopolization (Section 4.17).

### 2.1 Append-Only Principles
- Ledger entries are strictly **insert-only**; never updated or deleted.
- Balance is computed dynamically: `SELECT SUM(amount) FROM token_ledger WHERE user_id = :userId`.
- Types:
  - `ALLOWANCE`: Monthly grant (+ tokens).
  - `HOLD`: Pre-authorization hold on booking approval (- tokens held).
  - `CHARGE`: Settlement on booking completion.
  - `REFUND`: Release of hold on valid early cancellation.

---

## 3. Simulated Invoicing (`/invoices`)

- All invoices generated carry `"demo": true`.
- Payments are marked simulated with explicit user notice: *"Simulated payment in sandbox. No money moved."*

## 4. Document & Spreadsheet Exports (`/exports`)

Section 4.22 defines reporting requirements:

| Endpoint | Format | Library | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/exports/bookings.xlsx` | Excel (`.xlsx`) | `exceljs` | Facility booking reports, user hours, statuses |
| `/exports/usage-report.xlsx` | Excel (`.xlsx`) | `exceljs` | Department & institutional equipment utilization |
| `/exports/incident-report/:id.pdf` | PDF (`.pdf`) | `pdfkit` | 1-page formal incident dossier with timeline & findings |
| `/exports/audit.csv` | CSV | Built-in | Filterable tamper-evident audit trail log |
